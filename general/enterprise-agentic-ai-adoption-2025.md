## Key Takeaways

- 73% of enterprises have agentic AI in production or advanced pilots in 2025 — up from 34% ~18 months earlier
- Shift from simple prompt-response systems to **autonomous digital co-workers** executing multi-step workflows with limited supervision
- The strategic question: no longer whether AI works, but how multiple AI agents coordinate inside real business processes
- Agentic AI differs from conventional AI by operating autonomously, maintaining state across sessions, and executing goal-directed workflows
