## Summary

Agentic workflows differ from basic chatbot interactions through three core characteristics: multi-stage process execution (gather → plan → execute → review), decision-making under uncertainty with adaptive course correction, and clarification-before-execution to resolve ambiguity. This framework distinguishes genuine agents from sophisticated prompt-response systems.

## Key Takeaways

- Real agents follow staged processes (context gathering → planning → sequential execution → self-review) rather than single-response patterns; they operate through systems, not just respond to messages
- Agents reason through ambiguity and ask clarifying questions instead of guessing; they adapt approach mid-execution if assumptions prove wrong, separating them from pattern-matching autocomplete
- Clarification-before-execution prevents misinterpretation of audience, format, scope, and tone; most bad AI output stems from unstated assumptions—agents treat initial requests as conversation starters, not complete specifications

## ArchonOS Applicability

ArchonOS agents must implement this three-pillar architecture: explicit planning phases with state tracking, uncertainty-aware decision logic that prompts for clarification rather than assuming intent, and staged execution with self-review loops. This prevents brittle automation and enables robust homelab task orchestration.
