## Key Takeaways
- Pair every local transaction with a deterministic compensating transaction that reverses its business effect.
- On failure, stop forward progress and run compensations in reverse order to restore consistency.
- Keep conversational reasoning separate from the immutable transaction ledger.
- After a rollback, send the agent a structured failure signal that identifies the violated constraint before it replans.
- Use distinct protocols for tool access, peer-agent collaboration, and orchestration across critical business boundaries.
