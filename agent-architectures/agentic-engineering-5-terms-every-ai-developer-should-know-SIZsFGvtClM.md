## Key Takeaways
- Use clear instructions and bring domain expertise to the task; communication can matter more than choosing the most capable model.
- For smaller models, include few-shot examples and chain-of-thought examples to guide the responses you want.
- Manage context quality by compacting or summarizing it before the context window is full.
- Use sub-agents for focused research or documentation checks, then bring their findings back into the main context.
- Use a harness that can run tools, verify results such as test outcomes, manage context, sandbox actions, and coordinate agents.

> ## Advice

> - For smaller models, include few-shot examples and chain-of-thought examples to guide the responses you want.
