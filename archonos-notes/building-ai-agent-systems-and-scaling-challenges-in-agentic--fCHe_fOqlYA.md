## TubeOnAI Summary
> Scaling agentic AI is presented as a systems design problem rather than a simple matter of adding more compute or relying on stronger models. A single agent can perform well on narrow tasks, but as scope expands, planning, tool selection, memory management, and reflection become more expensive and less reliable, causing latency and cost to grow non-linearly. The argument emphasizes that errors in autonomous systems propagate across time and memory, as shown by the travel-booking example where "Washington" is misread as Washington, DC and the entire workflow is built on that faulty assumption. The proposed solution is to decompose monolithic agents into multi-agent systems with bounded responsibilities, then deliberately balance horizontal scaling through specialized agents against vertical scaling through more capable individual agents.
