## TubeOnAI Summary
> 💡 Multi-agent orchestration refers to a system where multiple specialized AI agents collaborate instead of relying on a single agent for all tasks.    – Each agent handles a distinct role or subtask, improving efficiency on complex workflows.    – Agents exchange data and distribute tasks among themselves to coordinate outcomes.  🔥 Why it is used    – A single AI agent may appear sufficient for simple tasks, but complex business processes often require coordinated specialization.    – Dividing work across agents can make automation faster and support more scalable decision-making.  ⚙️ Enterprise relevance    – Multi-agent architectures are being adopted in modern enterprise AI solutions.    – The implied business value is better handling of large-scale, multi-step operations through coordinated AI systems.
