## TubeOnAI Summary
> Hermes is a free, open-source agent that can handle recurring work across meetings, email, monitoring, client chats, shared memory, remote access, and morning planning. The creator’s central advice is to give it real jobs, while requiring human approval before it sends messages or spends money.

Advice
- Connect a meeting-notes tool and calendar to Hermes, then have it extract action items and owners and create assigned tasks in Notion; assign unclear items to yourself and flag them.
- Have Hermes flag important emails and draft replies in your voice, but keep sending approval-based to avoid incorrect information going to leads.
- Set clear conditions for website or external-page monitoring so alerts stay useful, and require your approval before any purchase.
- Use client-chat monitoring to identify quiet or unhappy clients, then contact them yourself, especially about sensitive matters.
- Set up shared memory across your tools if you want them to carry context between conversations.
- Send Hermes requests from your phone when you need access to your home computer’s files or tools.
- Schedule a morning brief with your calendar, important overnight emails, and key numbers; tell Hermes your top priority and let it work as far as it safely can without spending money or sending anything externally.
