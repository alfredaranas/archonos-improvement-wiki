## Key Takeaways
- **💡 Single chatbots are presented as insufficient for handling complex business tasks** — The core limitation implied is that one general-purpose bot cannot reliably manage multi-step, specialized, or cross-functional workflows alone.
- **🔥 Multi-agent systems are identified as a major AI trend for 2026** — These systems use multiple specialized AI agents instead of a single model.
- **🔄 Orchestration is the key mechanism that enables agents to work together** — Agents communicate with one another, coordinate responsibilities, and pass work between roles.
- **🏗️ Multi-agent orchestration is framed as an architecture problem** — The focus is not only on individual models, but on the system design that connects them.
- **🧩 Business relevance centers on automation of complex workflows** — Multi-agent setups are positioned for tasks that require collaboration, specialization, and oversight.
