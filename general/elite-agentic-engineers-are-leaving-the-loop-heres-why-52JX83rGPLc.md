## Key Takeaways
- Ask what work can run without you next, and replace yourself as the trigger with events such as schedules, logs, tickets, or user actions.
- Move toward autonomy incrementally: first have agents prepare work for your approval, then build systems you can monitor rather than constantly direct.
- Give long-running agents their own sandbox and computing resources so they can keep working independently of your device and survive restarts.
- Build a clear application programming interface layer to connect triggers to agents.
- Give durable agents memory so they can track work, ask questions, and resume after interruptions.
