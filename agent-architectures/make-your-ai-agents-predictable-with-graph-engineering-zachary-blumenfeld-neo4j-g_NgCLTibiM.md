## Key Takeaways
- Compile written procedures, policies, and successful decision traces into graph-shaped skills with explicit steps and routing.
- Move policy rules and routing into execution scripts instead of asking the agent to decide every step.
- Use a decision model for cases that pass hard policy checks but still need a risk-tier or approval decision.
- Route uncertain cases to human review, and validate or lint reports before they are circulated.
- Persist runs and inspect their steps, branches, approvals, and denials to support governance and detect behavior over time.
